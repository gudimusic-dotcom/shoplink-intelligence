# Vision

## Purpose

SHOPLINK Intelligence is designed to become the central operational platform of MobileGo.

The goal is to replace fragmented workflows spread across multiple systems with one intelligent, integrated platform that supports the company's daily operations.

Rather than functioning as a reporting dashboard, the system will actively assist employees in performing their work, making decisions, monitoring business performance and managing operational processes.

The platform is intended to become the digital workplace where every employee starts their day.

---

# Mission

The mission of SHOPLINK Intelligence is to provide every employee with the information, tools and tasks required to perform their work efficiently from a single interface.

The system should reduce manual work, improve visibility across departments and enable management to make faster and more informed decisions.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The long-term objective is to build a scalable Business Intelligence and Operations Platform that grows together with the company.

The architecture must support future expansion without requiring fundamental redesign.

Future versions may include:

- AI-powered business analysis
- Predictive inventory planning
- Automated purchasing recommendations
- Marketing performance optimisation
- Financial forecasting
- Supplier performance evaluation
- Customer behaviour analysis
- SaaS deployment for external companies

The first versions will focus exclusively on MobileGo's internal operations.

---

# Business Problems

The project addresses several existing operational challenges.

## Fragmented Information

Business data is currently distributed across multiple systems.

Employees often need to switch between different applications to complete a single task.

This increases complexity, reduces productivity and increases the risk of mistakes.

---

## Lack of Operational Visibility

Management lacks a single real-time overview of company performance.

Important information is often discovered too late.

The platform should continuously monitor business performance and highlight important changes.

---

## Manual Processes

Many daily activities require repetitive manual work.

Examples include:

- preparing reports
- analysing sales
- checking inventory
- monitoring complaints
- assigning tasks

These activities should gradually become automated.

---

## Reactive Decision Making

Current decisions are often based on manually collected information.

The objective is to move towards proactive decision support where the system identifies problems before they become critical.

---

# Strategic Objectives

The platform should help achieve the following business goals.

## Increase Efficiency

Reduce time spent on repetitive administrative work.

---

## Increase Profitability

Provide better visibility into margins, costs, pricing and product performance.

---

## Improve Inventory Management

Reduce dead stock.

Prevent stock shortages.

Support purchasing decisions using historical sales data.

---

## Improve Customer Service

Centralise complaint handling.

Reduce complaint processing time.

Provide complete complaint history.

---

## Improve Management Decisions

Present accurate, up-to-date business information.

Provide AI-generated recommendations where appropriate.

---

# Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

- employees perform most of their daily work inside SHOPLINK Intelligence;
- managers rely on the platform for operational decisions;
- manual reporting is significantly reduced;
- complaint handling becomes fully traceable;
- inventory decisions become data-driven;
- operational transparency increases across departments.

---

# Non-Goals

The following are explicitly outside the scope of the platform.

- Public e-commerce storefront
- ERP replacement
- Accounting software replacement
- CRM replacement
- Marketplace replacement

The platform integrates with external systems rather than replacing them.

---

# Guiding Principle

Every new feature must answer one question:

**Does this feature help MobileGo operate more efficiently?**

If the answer is no, the feature should not be implemented.
